

# Logeo-Seine-Internship

**Financial analysis of real estate projects & construction site monitoring at a social housing provider.**

This repository contains the deliverables, analyses and documentation produced during my first-year engineering internship at the **Technical Department of Logeo Seine** (Groupe ActionLogement), in Le Havre, France, from June 16 to July 16, 2025.


## 📝 Project Overview

The Technical Department of a social housing provider manages new construction, energy-efficient renovation and the acquisition/upgrading of existing buildings. My internship combined two complementary aspects: office-based financial analysis and on-site operational monitoring.

1. **Multi-project financial analysis:** Built Excel dashboards and comparative charts for three types of operations — new construction, thermal renovation and office-to-residential conversion — with cost per work package and cost per housing unit.
2. **Construction site monitoring:** Took part in site visits and coordination meetings (OPC scheduling/coordination manager, architect, trades), monitored progress and tracked snag resolution before handover.
3. **Handover logistics:** Labelled and managed keys and access badges, prepared units for handover to the letting agencies.

This work gave the technical team a synthetic, comparable view of the costs of its recent operations and helped secure handover preparation.

## ✨ Key Features

* **3 operations analysed** in detail (new build, renovation, acquisition/upgrade) + summary tables covering all recent operations.
* **Cost per work package and per unit** calculated for each operation, including contract amendments.
* **Growing autonomy:** from observing site meetings to independently running an on-site intervention (opening units, coordinating contractors, resolving an unexpected issue).
* **Logistical rigour:** keys sorted per unit and badges managed to secure handover to the agencies.
